OPEN THE INVITATION
1. Extract this ZIP completely.
2. Keep index.html, styles.css, script.js and the assets folder together.
3. Open index.html in a browser on a computer.
4. Tap/click the champagne bottle to enter and start the music.

TO SHARE WITH GUESTS
Upload the contents of this folder to your static website host, keeping the
folder structure intact. Send that page's link to guests. A hosted link opened
in Safari or Chrome is the easiest way to enjoy it on a phone; a ZIP preview
inside a messaging or Files app may not run interactive pages.

THE EXPERIENCE
• Tap the bottle: the cork pops and champagne fizz fills the screen.
• Scroll to the drinks box. Rub the gold label with a finger or mouse.
• The box opens with a champagne splash, confetti, gold light bursts and
  expanding rings. The date bottle springs up and gently floats.
• Scrolling ends at the second chapter until the box-opening animation
  finishes. The party details, dress code and closing then unlock.
• The countdown starts as the date is revealed.
• The tap-to-reveal alternative also works with a keyboard.
• The directions button opens the exact Google Maps link you supplied.
• The venue details and dance floor share one continuous background.
• The central power button switches on sweeping colour spotlights, laser
  fans, floor lights, light washes, disco sparkles and a confetti burst.
  Switching it off clears the moving particles and dims the lighting.
• Sound and motion controls remain available at the top of the page.

MUSIC
Your complete, original attached MP3 is included as assets/party-music.mp3.
It loops and starts after the opening bottle tap, because mobile browsers
generally require an interaction before playing sound. Use Sound on/off to
pause or resume it. The track pauses when the page goes into the background.
